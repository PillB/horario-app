## PART 5 — FREEMIUM CONVERSION BENCHMARKS

Freemium self-serve products typically see 3–5% conversion rates, with exceptional performers reaching 6–8%. Sales-assisted freemiums perform better at 5–7% average and 10–15% for top performers.

| Model | Avg. Conversion | Top Performers |
|-------|----------------|----------------|
| Freemium (self-serve) | 3–5% | 6–8% |
| Freemium (sales-assisted) | 5–7% | 10–15% |
| Free Trial (opt-in) | 15–25% | 30%+ |
| Free Trial (opt-out) | 50–60% | 70%+ |

**Implication for Horario:** The "1 free AI schedule/IP/month" model resembles a **rate-limited freemium**. Expect ~3–5% organic conversion. To reach 6–8%, pair with in-app upgrade prompts tied to the "uncovered revenue loss" metric (high urgency trigger) and proactive sales outreach for accounts scheduling >3 sessions/month.
